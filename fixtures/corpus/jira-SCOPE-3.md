An owner enters one or more email addresses and picks an access level. Each
address receives an invitation email with a link that expires after seven days.

An invitation to an address that already has an account joins that account to
the workspace directly. An invitation to an unknown address creates a pending
invitation that becomes a membership when the person signs up.

Invitations are sent through the existing transactional email service.
